### 🏥 Insurance Cost Analysis
> *What percentage of total insurance costs come from high-cost claimants?*

![BigQuery](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google%20BigQuery-4285F4?style=flat-square&logo=googlebigquery&logoColor=white)
![SQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/SQL-336791?style=flat-square&logo=postgresql&logoColor=white)
![Looker Studio](https://img.shields.io/badge/Looker%20Studio-4285F4?style=flat-square&logo=looker&logoColor=white)

This project analyzes a medical insurance dataset to identify how much of total insurance spending is driven by the top 10% of highest-cost claimants — a critical metric for insurers and healthcare administrators.

**Key findings:**
- 🔴 Top 10% of claimants account for **~34% of total insurance costs**
- 📍 Southeast region drives the **highest regional spending**
- 🧬 Obese BMI group has the **most high-cost claimants**
- 👴 Costs peak significantly in the **60+ age group**

**What I built:**
- 6 SQL views in BigQuery covering KPIs, cost splits, and segment breakdowns
- Interactive Looker Studio dashboard with scorecards, bar charts, pie chart, and geo map
